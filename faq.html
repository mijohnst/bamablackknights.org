Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers for parents who are new to the West Point experience or looking to get more involved with the club.

Questions Families Ask

What is the West Point Parents Club of Alabama?

We are a parent-led club focused on supporting Alabama families connected to the United States Military Academy. Our goal is to offer connection, information, and community throughout the West Point journey.

Who can join the club?

Our club is intended for parents and family members of cadets and graduates associated with the Academy. We welcome families at every stage of the cadet journey.

Do I need to be a member to attend events?

Many club events are open to interested families, but membership helps keep families connected to club communications, volunteer opportunities, and the broader community.

How can I get involved?

You can join the club, volunteer for events, support committee efforts, or simply connect with other families through club communications and gatherings.

How do I stay informed about club updates?

We encourage parents to sign up for email updates, follow the club on social media, and check the events and resources pages here on the website.

Is this website a replacement for the member portal?

No. This website is designed as a public, informative resource for families and the broader community. A future member portal may support additional internal functions like member-only access and event management.

How can I contact the club?

You can use the contact page to reach out via email or message form. We are happy to help answer questions and connect you with the right club leadership member.
