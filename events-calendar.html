            Class of 2030

            Key Milestones

            Key dates for the Class of 2030. Exact times and locations are sent directly to families in the official reporting letter on your cadet candidate's portal — for the most current schedule, visit westpoint.edu/parents.

            29 Jun 2026Reception Day (R-Day) — new cadets report to West Point, check in at Eisenhower Hall
            29 Jun 2026Oath Ceremony, The Plain — evening; bleacher seating available for families
            Early–mid Aug 2026March Back — the roughly 12-mile ruck march ending Cadet Basic Training; families can line the streets
            15 Aug 2026Acceptance Day Parade, The Plain — new cadets formally join the Corps of Cadets
            17 Aug 2026Fall semester classes begin
            Spring 2027Parent-Plebe Weekend — first chance for families to reconnect since R-Day
            All events and dates are subject to change. Always verify with official West Point communications and your candidate's portal.
            R-Day

            Reception Day (R-Day)

            Follow the parking and shuttle instructions in your cadet candidate's official reporting letter. New cadets check in at Eisenhower Hall, receive uniforms and haircuts, and get roughly 60 seconds to say goodbye before training begins.

            What to Expect

            • Check-in and processing at Eisenhower Hall
            • Information booths from West Point support offices
            • Oath Ceremony on The Plain that evening — the moment new cadets formally join West Point

            Staying Connected During Beast

            New cadets cannot receive packages during Cadet Basic Training ("Beast") — only letters in plain white envelopes. Write your first letter early so it's waiting when mail catches up. Our Parents Club coordinates care package drives for once your cadet can receive them.
