            Who We Are

            The West Point Parents Club of Alabama is a volunteer-run, non-profit organization dedicated to supporting Alabama families whose sons and daughters have earned the honor of attending the United States Military Academy in West Point, New York.

            We serve as a bridge for families at home and their cadets at the Academy, working in coordination with Academy officials and liaison officers — providing guidance, community, and resources from the moment of appointment through commissioning day.

            The Cadet Journey

            Four Years at West Point

            Every cadet moves through four class years on the way to commissioning, each with its own name, traditions, and milestones. Here's a quick guide for families new to the journey.

            4th Class · Year One

            Plebe

            Reception Day & Beast

            Reception Day (R-Day) marks the start of Cadet Basic Training, known as Beast Barracks. Plebes learn the fundamentals of military life — drill, inspections, Academy history, and the Cadet Prayer.

            3rd Class · Year Two

            Yearling

            Recognition

            Recognition each spring marks a Plebe's transition into the Corps as an upperclass cadet. Yearlings take on heavier academics and begin mentoring the newest class.

            2nd Class · Year Three

            Cow

            Branch Selection Begins

            Cows step into leadership roles across the Corps and begin the branch selection process that will shape their future Army career field.

            1st Class · Year Four

            Firstie

            Graduation & Commissioning

            The final year builds toward Ring Weekend, Branch Night, and Graduation — the moment a cadet becomes a second lieutenant in the U.S. Army.

            A West Point Tradition

            Ring Weekend

            Each fall, rising Firsties receive their class ring in one of West Point's most photographed traditions — a milestone families look forward to as much as the cadets do.

            📅

            When

            Held during a cadet's Cow-to-Firstie fall, roughly one year before graduation.

            💎

            The Ring

            Every class designs its own ring, incorporating its class crest, motto, and Academy symbols.

            🏆

            The Tradition

            Newly-ringed cadets famously "dunk" the ring in a beverage-filled mug and drink until it can be retrieved with their teeth.
