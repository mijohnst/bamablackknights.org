Who We Are

About Our Club

The USAFA Parents Club of Alabama is a volunteer-run, non-profit organization dedicated to supporting Alabama families whose sons and daughters have earned the honor of attending the United States Air Force Academy in Colorado Springs, Colorado.

We serve as a bridge for families at home and their cadets at the Academy, working in coordination with Academy officials and liaison officers — providing guidance, community, and resources from the moment of appointment through commissioning day.

Membership & Donations

Annual Membership

$75 / year

Membership dues cover cadet support activities, club operations, and care packages for Alabama cadets throughout their Academy journey.

As a member, you'll be part of a community of Alabama families who share the unique experience of having a son or daughter at the Academy — with access to resources and a network of parents who understand the journey firsthand.

Paid members also get access to our member portal, where you can vote for (and run for) club leadership, join committees, RSVP to events, share event photos, and connect with other families through our member directory.

🎖️ Paid members will receive a club identification badge

Donations
