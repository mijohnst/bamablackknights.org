About the Club

We are a parent-led support network for families connected to the United States Military Academy, creating community, sharing information, and helping Alabama families stay connected through every stage of the West Point journey.

Our Mission

The West Point Parents Club of Alabama exists to strengthen the connection between cadet families, support one another through the unique challenges and triumphs of the West Point experience, and provide timely resources, information, and encouragement for parents across the state.

We believe that strong family support helps build strong cadets. Through meaningful events, practical communication, and trusted leadership, we work to create a supportive environment for parents and families at every stage of the journey.

Who We Serve

Our club is intended for parents and family members of cadets and graduates associated with the United States Military Academy. We welcome those who are new to the West Point journey as well as those who have been part of the Academy community for years.

Whether your cadet is beginning the admissions process, entering the Academy, adjusting to cadet life, or preparing for graduation, our club is here to support you and help you stay connected to both the West Point community and the Alabama family network.

Why This Matters

The West Point experience is unique, and families often benefit from a support system that understands what they are going through. Our club provides that network of encouragement, shared experience, and practical information.

We are proud to help Alabama families stay connected to one another while strengthening the broader West Point community.
